  • เคสเป็นสถานการณ์สมมติเพื่อการฝึก ห้ามใส่ข้อมูลของผู้ป่วยจริง

เข้าสู่ระบบ

Case Studio

Where the authors of the Critical Care nursing training game (Admins and nurses) write and check its cases, then publish them to the app or export them as one file the app imports.

  • Only people an admin has given an account; nobody signs up.
  • Sign in with a Google account, or with a username and password.
  • The Critical Care app asks the studio only for the newest published cases: nothing about its players is sent to the studio.
  • Cases are made-up training scenarios: never enter a real patient's details.
